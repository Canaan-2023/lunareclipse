# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

- Be friendly and inclusive; respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus on the issue.
- Address the work, not the person; no personal attacks or derogation.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, trolling, insults, public or private harassment, or publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through a private channel listed in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), by email to **canaanmonika@foxmail.com**, or by Bilibili DM (雪花莲的超星际远征). Reports will be reviewed and handled.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1.

# 贡献者公约

## 我们的承诺

为营造开放、友好的环境，贡献者和维护者承诺：无论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残疾、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何，参与本项目的每个人都不受骚扰。

## 我们的标准

- 友善、包容，尊重不同观点与经验。
- 接受建设性批评，聚焦问题本身。
- 对事不对人，不做人身攻击或贬损。

不可接受的行为包括：使用性化的语言或图像、人身攻击、挑衅、侮辱或贬损、公开或私下骚扰、未经许可发布他人隐私信息。

## 处理

遇到违反本公约的行为，可通过 [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) 列出的私密渠道、发送邮件至 **canaanmonika@foxmail.com**，或在 B 站私信「雪花莲的超星际远征」联系维护者。维护者会对投诉进行审查与处理。

本公约改编自 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) 2.1。
